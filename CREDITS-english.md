# Oraclia · Credits and Provenance

Oraclia is developed under the initiative and conceptual direction of Judit Llop Padrós, through a process of co-creation with ChatGPT and the Oraclia GPT.

## Scope of the historical record

As Judit explains, a complete record of authorship and decisions is not available from the beginning of the project. The preserved materials may document parts of the process, but must not be presented as an exhaustive record.

Any later reconstruction must be identified as such and distinguish the available documents from recollections or unverified attributions. Documentation of new decisions must distinguish human contributions, AI suggestions, transformations, what has been discarded and what remains open.

## Provenance of this note

This note was added on 15 November 2026 and prepared with ChatGPT at Judit’s request, following the clarification that a complete record is not available from the beginning. It is not a contemporaneous record of the project’s early stages.
